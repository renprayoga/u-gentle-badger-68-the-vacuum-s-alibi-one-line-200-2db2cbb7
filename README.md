One line, at most 200 characters, in which a robot vacuum explains why it
missed a spot — for the [make.fun bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/u-gentle-badger-68-the-vacuum-s-alibi-one-line-200-2db2cbb7).

## The entry

> Spot report: dust located, identified as a crumb from your 2 a.m. snack, left as evidence. I do not clean crime scenes.

119 characters, one line.

## Why this line

- **Deadpan robot voice:** it opens with a machine-style "Spot report:" and
  speaks in flat bureaucratic verbs — located, identified, left.
- **Surprise:** the dust is a crumb from the owner's 2 a.m. snack, and the
  vacuum treats it as forensic evidence instead of dirt.
- **Tightness:** 119 of 200 characters, every word earns its place, and the
  punch — "I do not clean crime scenes." — sits at the very end.

## How to run

Python 3.13, standard library only, no network, no randomness:

```sh
python3 main.py
```

It validates the line (one line, 1–200 characters, not only whitespace)
and writes it to `/output/entry.txt`.
